# Scaletta: talk EGU (10 minuti domande comprese, regola EGU da paper-scrittura/conferenze.md)

1. **CIMA_TITLE**: [Title of the talk: the main finding of the paper in one line]
   - Corpo: [Paper reference: authors, journal, year, DOI]
   - Note: Ten minutes including questions: aim for eight minutes of talk.

2. **CIMA_CONTENT**: [Problem and why it matters, in one sentence]
   - Corpo: Why this matters / [Problem, with one number from the paper and its source] / [Who is affected]
   - Note: 30 seconds. One fact, not a review of the literature.

3. **CIMA_CONTENT**: [Gap: what current methods cannot do]
   - Corpo: What is missing / [Gap stated in the paper's introduction] / [Our question]
   - Note: 45 seconds. The gap as the paper states it.

4. **CIMA_CONTENT_2COL**: [What we did: method in one sentence]
   - Corpo: Method / [Model or approach] / [Key step] / [Validation strategy] / [Workflow diagram] / [to be inserted from the paper]
   - Note: One minute. Details go to backup.

5. **CIMA_CONTENT_2COL**: [Data and study area: basin, period, events]
   - Corpo: Data and area / [Basin and size] / [Period and number of events] / [Data sources with providers] / [Map of the study area] / [from the paper, with caption]
   - Note: One minute.

6. **CIMA_CONTENT_2COL**: [Main result, stated with its number: e.g. metric X improved from A to B]
   - Corpo: Main result / [Metric, value, period] / [Compared with: baseline] / [Key figure from the paper] / [Figure n of the paper, axes readable]
   - Note: Two minutes. This is the slide people photograph: numbers only from the paper.

7. **CIMA_CONTENT**: [Second result, with its number]
   - Corpo: Second result / [Metric, value] / [What it adds to the main result]
   - Note: One minute.

8. **CIMA_CONTENT**: [Limits: where the method does not hold]
   - Corpo: Limits / [Limit 1 from the paper's discussion] / [Limit 2]
   - Note: One minute. Do not skip: the questions will start here.

9. **CIMA_CONTENT**: [Take-home message in one sentence]
   - Corpo: [Message] / [Paper DOI] / [Contact: name, email]
   - Note: 30 seconds. End on the message, not on 'Thank you'.

10. **CIMA_BACKUP**: [Method details: calibration, parameters, validation]
   - Corpo: [From the paper's methods section]
   - Note: For method questions.

11. **CIMA_BACKUP**: [Full results table]
   - Corpo: [Table n of the paper]
   - Note: For questions on single events or stations.
