# ANTICIPATE: Anticipatory flood risk management in small Mediterranean catchments

Call: HORIZON-CL3-2026-DRS-01-99. Type of action: RIA.

## 1. Excellence

### 1.1 Objectives and ambition

ANTICIPATE will develop an anticipatory flood risk management framework for
small Mediterranean catchments. The project goes well beyond the state of the
art in hydrological forecasting, delivering a highly innovative and
groundbreaking approach to impact-based warning.

Objectives:

- O1: develop an integrated nowcasting and ensemble forecasting chain for
  catchments with response times below six hours;
- O2: co-design impact-based thresholds with civil protection authorities;
- O3: demonstrate the framework in operational conditions;
- O4: increase the uptake of open hydrological data across Europe.

### 1.2 Methodology

The methodology combines radar nowcasting, ensemble numerical weather
prediction and a distributed hydrological model. The chain will be
implemented in three case study catchments and validated against past
events. Thresholds will be derived from impact data collected with the
authorities.

## 2. Impact

### 2.1 Project pathways towards impact

ANTICIPATE will have a significant impact on society, improving the
resilience of Mediterranean regions and saving lives. The project results
will be adopted by civil protection authorities across Europe, and will
contribute to the European Green Deal.

Key performance indicator: the number of authorities using the ANTICIPATE
products will reach 15 by the end of the project.

Through its training programme, ANTICIPATE will build the capacity of more
than 200 civil protection officers to use impact-based forecasts.

### 2.2 Measures to maximise impact

The project will set up a website, a logo, a newsletter and social media
channels. A final conference will be organised in Brussels. Scientific
results will be published in open access journals.
