## HORIZON-CL3-2026-DRS-01-99: Anticipatory flood risk management in small Mediterranean catchments

**Call**: HORIZON-CL3-2026-DRS-01
**Type of Action**: HORIZON Research and Innovation Actions
**Deadline**: 19 November 2026 17:00:00 Brussels time
**Indicative budget**: EUR 18.00 million for this topic. The Commission
estimates that an EU contribution of around EUR 6.00 million would allow these
outcomes to be addressed appropriately. Around 3 projects are expected to be
funded.

### Specific conditions

The following exceptions apply to the General Annexes:

- The threshold for the criterion 'Impact' is 4 instead of 3. The overall
  threshold remains 10.
- The page limit for the application is 30 pages.
- Proposals must include at least one operational civil protection authority
  among the beneficiaries.

### Expected Outcome

Projects are expected to contribute to all of the following outcomes:

- Improved anticipatory capacity of civil protection authorities in small and
  fast-responding Mediterranean catchments, with lead times extended beyond
  current operational practice.
- Validated impact-based forecasting services co-designed with end users and
  demonstrated in at least two operational contexts in different Member
  States or Associated Countries.
- Increased uptake of open hydrological data and interoperable early warning
  products across national and regional authorities.

### Scope

Proposals should develop and demonstrate anticipatory flood risk management
approaches for catchments with response times below six hours, where current
early warning systems provide limited actionable lead time.

Proposals are expected to address all of the following:

- integration of nowcasting, ensemble hydrological forecasting and
  impact-based thresholds;
- co-design with civil protection and local authorities, documented through
  structured user engagement throughout the project;
- demonstration in real operational conditions, including at least one event
  exercise with the participating authorities;
- interoperability with existing European and national early warning
  infrastructures, including compliance with relevant open data standards.

Proposals must dedicate a specific work package to clustering activities with
other projects funded under this call and under the Destination.

Proposals should address the gender dimension in the design of warning
communication.

This topic requires the effective contribution of social sciences and
humanities disciplines.
