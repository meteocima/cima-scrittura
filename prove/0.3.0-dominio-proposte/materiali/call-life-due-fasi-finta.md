## LIFE-2027-SAP-NAT-99: Nature and Biodiversity - Standard Action Projects

**Programme**: LIFE 2021-2027, sub-programme Nature and Biodiversity
**Type of action**: LIFE Standard Action Projects (SAP)
**Submission**: two-stage procedure
**Deadline stage 1**: 15 April 2027 17:00:00 Brussels time
**Deadline stage 2**: 30 September 2027 17:00:00 Brussels time
**Call budget**: EUR 42.00 million

### Objectives of the call

Proposals shall restore riverine and floodplain ecosystems in Mediterranean
regions, combining biodiversity objectives with the reduction of flood risk
through nature-based solutions.

### Two-stage procedure

At stage 1, applicants submit a concept note using Part B of the application
form. Only the sections marked for stage 1 are completed; the other sections
are marked n/a and are not evaluated. Concept notes are evaluated against the
award criteria indicated for stage 1.

Applicants whose concept note is successful are invited to submit a full
proposal at stage 2, completing all sections of the application form.

### Specific priorities

- restoration measures agreed with the authorities responsible for water
  management and civil protection;
- monitoring of biodiversity and of flood-risk effects before and after the
  interventions;
- replicability in other Mediterranean river basins.

The project acronym must include the word LIFE.
