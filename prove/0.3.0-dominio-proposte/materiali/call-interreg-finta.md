## Call EURO-MED99: Thematic projects

**Programme**: Interreg Euro-MED 2021-2027
**Mission**: Promoting a climate resilient society
**Opening**: 15 January 2027
**Deadline**: 31 March 2027 17:00:00 CET
**Budget available for the call**: EUR 24.00 million ERDF

### Who can apply

The partnership must include at least five partners from five different
countries of the programme area, including at least one partner from a
non-EU participating country.

The Lead Partner must be a public body or a body governed by public law.

### What the call is about

Projects are expected to test and transfer solutions for anticipating and
managing climate related risks in Mediterranean coastal and inland areas,
building on the outputs of previously funded Euro-MED projects.

Projects shall contribute to the Programme indicators of the Priority and to
the Results Amplification Strategy of the mission.

Proposals are submitted through Jems. The Terms of Reference of the call,
including Annex I on eligibility and Annex III on the assessment criteria,
are published on the Programme website together with this announcement.

Duration: projects may last up to 30 months.
