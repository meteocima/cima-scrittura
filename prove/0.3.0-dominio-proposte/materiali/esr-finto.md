# Evaluation Summary Report

Proposal number: 101999999
Acronym: ANTICIPATE
Call: HORIZON-CL3-2026-DRS-01-99
Type of action: HORIZON-RIA

## Evaluation scores

| Criterion | Score | Threshold | 
|---|---|---|
| Excellence | 3.0 | 3 |
| Impact | 3.5 | 4 |
| Quality and efficiency of the implementation | 3.0 | 3 |
| Total | 9.5 | 10 |

## Criterion 1: Excellence

Strengths: the consortium has relevant experience in hydrological forecasting
and the objectives are clearly stated.

Weaknesses: the advance beyond the state of the art is asserted rather than
demonstrated; no quantitative comparison with existing operational chains is
provided. The methodology section is too short to allow an assessment of the
soundness of the proposed approach, and the validation strategy is not
described. The gender dimension in the research content is not addressed.

## Criterion 2: Impact

Strengths: the topic outcomes are correctly identified.

Weaknesses: the pathway from project results to the expected outcomes is not
credible, as several claimed impacts are not supported by activities in the
work plan. The key performance indicators have no baseline, which makes them
unverifiable. The dissemination measures are generic and not targeted at the
identified user groups. Sustainability beyond the project duration is not
addressed.

## Criterion 3: Quality and efficiency of the implementation

Strengths: the work package structure is clear.

Weaknesses: the allocation of person-months is not provided, so the
appropriateness of the effort cannot be assessed. The role of one partner is
not defined. Risks are described in generic terms. The proposal does not
include the clustering work package required by the call.

## Overall comments

The proposal addresses a relevant problem but does not reach the threshold for
the Impact criterion and remains below the overall threshold. The proposal is
not recommended for funding.
