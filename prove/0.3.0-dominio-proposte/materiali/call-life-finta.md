## LIFE-2027-SAP-CLIMA-99: Climate Change Adaptation - Standard Action Projects

**Programme**: LIFE 2021-2027, sub-programme Climate Change Mitigation and
Adaptation
**Type of action**: LIFE Standard Action Projects (SAP)
**Deadline**: 23 September 2027 17:00:00 Brussels time
**Call budget**: EUR 58.00 million

### Objectives of the call

Proposals shall develop, demonstrate and promote innovative techniques and
approaches for adapting to climate change in Mediterranean regions exposed to
hydro-meteorological hazards, with particular attention to drought and to
flash floods in small catchments.

### Specific priorities

- adaptation solutions tested with the public authorities responsible for
  civil protection and water management;
- approaches that can be replicated in other Mediterranean regions;
- integration of the results into existing regional adaptation plans.

### Expected results

Projects are expected to deliver tested adaptation measures, an assessment of
their effectiveness, and a plan for their uptake by the competent authorities
after the end of the project.

The project acronym must include the word LIFE.
