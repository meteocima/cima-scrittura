## UCPM-2027-TRACK1-99: Technical Assistance for Disaster Risk Management

**Programme**: Union Civil Protection Mechanism
**Type of action**: UCPM Project Grants
**Deadline**: 14 April 2027 17:00:00 Brussels time
**Call budget**: EUR 9.00 million. The EU contribution may cover up to 90% of
eligible costs.

### Objectives

Proposals shall strengthen the capacity of national and sub-national
authorities to anticipate and manage disaster risk, in line with the Disaster
Resilience Goals and the Preparedness Union Strategy.

### Priorities

- improving risk assessment and anticipatory action for hydro-meteorological
  hazards;
- strengthening cross-border cooperation among Participating States;
- supporting the uptake of results by the authorities responsible for civil
  protection.

### Consortium

Proposals must be submitted by a consortium of at least three applicants from
three different Participating States.
