## HORIZON-EIC-2027-PATHFINDEROPEN-99: EIC Pathfinder Open

**Programme**: Horizon Europe, European Innovation Council
**Type of Action**: HORIZON-EIC Grant
**Deadline**: 12 May 2027 17:00:00 Brussels time
**Indicative budget**: EUR 120.00 million. The maximum EU contribution per
project is EUR 3.00 million.

### Scope

EIC Pathfinder Open supports the early-stage development of future
technologies, with no predefined thematic priorities. Proposals must pursue a
science-towards-technology breakthrough with the potential to create new
markets or address global challenges.

Projects are expected to reach proof of principle of the envisioned
technology, starting from the very early stages of research.

Proposals may be submitted by a consortium of at least three independent legal
entities from three different Member States or Associated Countries, or by a
single legal entity established in a Member State or Associated Country.

### What is expected

Applicants are expected to describe a long-term vision of a radically new
technology, the science-towards-technology breakthrough that the project would
achieve, and the interdisciplinary approach required.

Proposals should set out concrete objectives towards proof of principle, a
high-risk/high-gain research approach, and the measures planned to protect
results and to involve the actors able to translate research into innovation.
