## HORIZON-EIC-2027-DEEPTECH-99: EIC support for deep-tech in climate resilience

**Programme**: Horizon Europe, European Innovation Council
**Type of Action**: HORIZON-EIC Grant
**Deadline**: 14 October 2027 17:00:00 Brussels time
**Indicative budget**: EUR 60.00 million

### Scope

The EIC supports breakthrough technologies that can strengthen Europe's
resilience to climate extremes, from early detection of hydro-meteorological
hazards to new materials for protective infrastructure.

This call is open to teams that want to take a promising technology further,
whatever its current stage, provided that the innovation is radical and that
the team can show a credible route towards its uptake.

Applicants may be single legal entities, including start-ups and SMEs, or
small consortia.

### What is expected

Applicants should describe the technology, the evidence gathered so far, the
next steps towards its use, and the team that will carry them out.

Details on the evaluation process, the criteria and the application template
are given in the EIC Work Programme.
